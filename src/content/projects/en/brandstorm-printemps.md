## Context

As Project Leader in L’Oréal Brandstorm, I developed PRINTEMPS, a personalised scalp-care device concept combining multispectral imaging with big-data training.

## My contribution

Defined the target audience as high-income women aged 21–58 in Tier-1 cities, focusing on time-efficiency needs. Designed a digital marketing strategy connecting social media and e-commerce, with participation informed by the IKEA effect.

Built an O+O (online + offline) service model with a smart assistant and product-preservation technology to improve the user journey.

## Deliverables

An AI-powered device concept, target-consumer positioning, digital marketing strategy and connected service model.
