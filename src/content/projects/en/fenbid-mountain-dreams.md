## Context

Executed a cause-marketing project with a budget of approximately ¥5 million, reinforcing a caring pain-management brand positioning.

## My contribution

Proposed a film concept positioning music as “digital ibuprofen”, with the product in a teacher’s first-aid kit to convey ongoing care. Managed script development, production execution and media distribution.

Coordinated the campaign concert across artist invitations, government approvals, venue selection and on-site recording.

## Results

The campaign generated 39M+ impressions and 5M video views and 860K engagements, with Tencent News media placements and livestreaming across four major music platforms.
