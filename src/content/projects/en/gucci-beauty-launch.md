## Context

During my Coty–Gucci Beauty internship, I worked on fragrance sales analysis, launch support and competitor monitoring.

## My contribution

Collected, cleaned and consolidated daily, weekly and monthly sales data across DS, EC and Tmall. Built SKU-level reports to review product performance and channel contribution.

Supported Gucci Flora through market-trend research, consumer research, competitor analysis, product messaging and concept development. Coordinated packaging, e-commerce product pages and gifts with design, supply chain and legal teams.

## Results

Extracted sales data with SQL and presented trends in Excel dashboards and identified stronger and slower-moving products, providing evidence for launch planning and channel resource allocation.
