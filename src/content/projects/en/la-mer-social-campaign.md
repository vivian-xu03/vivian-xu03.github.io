## Context

During my La Mer product marketing internship, I owned end-to-end marketing campaigns for the Treatment Lotion Collection (Lotion & Infusion) across social briefs, creator selection, media placement and business reviews.

## My contribution

Designed KOL / KOC / RC approaches around consumer occasions, product propositions and content. Built competitor social tracking, produced 8 analysis reports and reviewed 4,500+ comments to identify pain points and usage occasions.

For the launch, analysed 50 campaign posts and tested product messages, identifying high-concentration ingredients as a core content direction.

## Results

The CNY campaign delivered 71 KOC posts, 3M+ impressions and a CPE of ¥5.4. The high-performing post rate increased from 51% to 72%. In launch content testing, one KOC post reached 500K+ impressions, with CPE as low as ¥3.5 and CPUV at ¥10.2.

## Content delivery

Built a shared Feishu review guide covering product messaging, checklists and revision examples. Worked with agencies on regular reviews to align messaging and streamline KOL/KOC delivery.
