## Context

At Publicis Groupe, I worked on Kao Laurier’s Double 11 integrated campaign, focusing on women’s usage occasions and emotional connection.

## My contribution

A review of the 618 results identified strong click-through performance in occasion-led assets, informing a summer-occasion creative direction. Developed a two-phase approach: build resonance during regular sales, then foreground reasons to believe during the promotional period.

## Results

The two-phase strategy supported a core-channel promotional ROI of 2.28. IP creative assets achieved a click-through rate of 17.04%.

## Creative optimisation

A/B testing showed that IP-collaboration assets outperformed live-action creative; value-driven “Save XX yuan” copy supported conversion. Proposed a “Summer Cooling Set” bundle to turn seasonal heat-related needs into purchase incentives.
