## Context

As Curator & President of the XJTLU Mindfulness Center, I led a 20-member team to organise the university’s largest mindfulness and mental-health summit.

## My contribution

Designed a four-day cross-sector programme with 18 panels and workshops spanning education, workplace resilience, AI ethics and family well-being.

Collaborated with 50+ international experts, including Chris Tamdjidi and senior scholars from Shanghai Jiao Tong University Medical School. Managed university-industry partnerships, sponsorships, fundraising and strategic negotiations with technology and healthcare firms.

## Results

Engaged 350+ participants and developed the student-led centre into a sustainable platform for social innovation in mental health.
